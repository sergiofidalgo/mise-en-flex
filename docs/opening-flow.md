# The opening — from launch to the office

*Spec, 2026-11-09, from the user's outline and their answers. References: the skyline mockup (`10_menu_skyline.svg`), the main menu mockup (`Art/Direction/concepts/2026-11-08_main-menu_new-player.png`), `office-scene.md`. Steps 2–9 built in `Office_Greybox`, the boot scene (2026-11-09).*

The first half-minute takes players from the city's streets up the boss's tower and into his floor, in one unbroken camera move. The menu and the office become one place: the game happens in the glowing penthouse you see from the street.

## The city

The fly-through, the menu's resting shot and the office have to be one city:
- **The tower's ground floor** (user, 2026-11-10, third pass):
  - **West, the tower's own lobby:** marble, a reception desk, the lift's glass shaft, the doorman at its door.
  - **East, the boss's restaurant, Mise en Flex,** behind clear glass. By the door, a host and the bar. Up six steps, a raised dining room right behind the windows, so it shows over the heads outside: cloths, candles, couples eating, someone photographing their plate, waiters with trays. At the back, an open kitchen with cooks at the hobs, steam and firelight.
  - **Its sign** is a dark fascia with brass edges carrying the logo's own lockup: "Mise en" in the gold serif italic and "FLEX" in the rounded cream face, between two crossed fork-and-spoon roundels, with "RESTAURANT & BAR" further along, a row of bulbs under it and a burgundy and cream awning over the dining windows (user, 2026-11-10, fourth pass: the name alone looked empty and its font didn't suit). The neon name, the five stars and the giant feed screen are gone: they didn't sit with the scene.
  - **Outside:** a queue behind a rope, a red carpet under a canopy ringed with bulbs, a couple posing, photographers, an influencer under a ring light, people going in and out.
  - **Delivery couriers** (user, 2026-11-10: a detail to keep in view once the menu is up; fifth pass: two scooters a little apart, and the couriers or at least their bags plain to see): two scooters on the kerb, right of where the menu hangs. The couriers wear one uniform, a lime cap and a big lime bag with a white chevron patch on a dark jacket: one sits on a scooter checking the next order, one stands with their back to the street so the bag faces us, one carries orders between the door and the kerb.
- **Phones first** (user, 2026-11-10: special emphasis on customers using their phones): a chunky modelled phone (a case in seven colours, a lit screen with an app on it, a camera island and the flash's LED on the back) held where it shows, since the characters' arms are short: selfies on a short stick out to the side, filming up in both hands, scrolling with the phone tilted at the chest and its glow on them. The flash is a flare at the phone's own LED.
- **Social pops, sparingly** (user, 2026-11-10: with all of them the scene was too busy): one phone in four pops at all, and rarely: four hearts, a like or rating pill, or a review bubble. Cards follow the phone from outside the character (a character's right arm is a mirrored copy, which showed a card back to front). Phones the menu would cover only send up hearts, so no card peeks out from behind it. Among the cards is a photo post, a polaroid of a plate with a tag: the one that used to be stuck to the menu's sign (user, 2026-11-10, fifth pass).
- **A crowd of individuals** (user, 2026-11-10, fourth pass): about four in ten people in the street wear one of the modelled hats (caps, headphones, a cowboy hat, a straw hat, a captain's cap, a plumed hat, party and propeller hats, a sun hat, a bandana, a headband); jackets, hoodies, vests, glasses, scarves and moustaches vary too, and hair comes short, long, in a bun, as an afro, as a fringe or not at all. The tower's staff keep to the odd cap or pair of headphones.
- **Lights that glow** (user, 2026-11-10, fourth pass: some were solid circles): string bulbs hang on wires, and they, the street lamps, the pendants and the stalls' bulbs each have a soft halo (`SH_Halo`, a glow that always faces the camera).
- **Another place to eat** in the podium west of the lobby, with a terrace.
- **Movement from edge to edge** (user, 2026-11-11, sixth pass: the approach felt a bit static): a courier on a scooter rides in from the east, pulls up at the kerb by the restaurant, waits for the order and rides off west; another rides straight through the other way; eight people walk the length of the street, some in a hurry. They ride and walk on the far side of the road, never close to the lens.
- **A street closed to traffic** between barriers, with a few market stalls among actual restaurants (user, 2026-11-10: too many stalls at first): the corner buildings either side of the avenue's mouth are restaurants with terraces; two stalls stand in the mouth and three along the street; bulbs are strung overhead; selfie pairs stand where the camera passes.

## The hand-over (step 7)

**One scene** (user, 2026-11-09): the office scene holds the street, the menu and the floor, so there's nothing to load between them. The climb runs from wherever the camera is drifting at the stop, up the tower's glass, and turns onto the office camera's pre-join wide shot. The first thing players see is the whole floor and the elevator they'll come out of.

## How it's built (2026-11-09, reworked 2026-11-10)

- **`OpeningSequence`** (Presentation/Office) runs the steps:
  1. save notice (first launch only, a `PlayerPrefs` flag; A skips it);
  2. 0.5 s fade in;
  3. the approach, 8 s (A skips, every time);
  4. the menu fades in a quarter of a second after the stop, the camera still;
  5. on choosing a campaign, the climb, 12 s (A skips to the floor);
  6. land.

  On landing, the follow camera takes over and joining opens. The menu's controller joins as player 1, coming out of the floor's elevator: the chef who rode up. The cab's chef hides and the empty cab sinks back down, switching itself off once it's out of sight. The street level and the tower's rooms switch off, since nobody sees them from the floor. The floor's player bar (`PlayerBarHud`) is hidden until this moment.
- **`OpeningChef`**: player 1's chef, in their look from the tailor (`ChefProfileFile`) and their colour, stands in the lift's cab from the start and rides up with it.
- **The camera path** is a centripetal Catmull-Rom curve through poses set in `OfficeBuilder.Opening.cs` (`OpeningPath`, tested). It's sampled by distance, so the pace is even however far apart the poses are. The approach is two poses looking the same way, so it's a straight push with no turn. It runs 2.8 m east of the tower's middle, so the restaurant fills the frame beside the menu. The climb goes straight up from where the camera stopped, about 16 m off the glass, then over the cornice.
- **The lift** (`OpeningElevator`) rides locked to the camera's height on the climb (`OpeningPath.LiftHeight`, no spring, so no lag), the chef in the middle of the frame. Its top stop is the boss's floor: it eases to a stop in the floor's elevator over the last few metres, and after landing sinks back down and switches off.
- **The helicopter** (`OpeningFlyby`) leaves its lap of the skyline halfway through the climb for one low pass just beyond the terrace rail, timed to the camera cresting the cornice and tipping onto the floor.
- **The haze** is thinner (170–650 m) in the street and over the menu, and eases back to the office's on the climb, so the tower reads from the street.
- **`OpeningHud`**: the black veil for fades, the save card (turning icon and two lines) and "A Skip" bottom right. Who has joined shows on the floor's own player bar once the camera lands.
- **`MainMenu`** starts hidden in the office (`_startHidden`). When the office listens to `Launching`, the menu hands over instead of loading the kitchen.
- **The menu's sign** is drawn piece by piece in code (user, 2026-11-10, fifth pass): the board on its rod, the logo cut from the mockup's picture (`blender/cut_menu_sign.py`), a REC light that blinks (a second on, half a second off) and a like count that goes up every two or three seconds with a bounce. The polaroid and the review that were stuck to it are gone; the street's phones pop those.
- **The modelled kit** (`Kit.cs`; `SM_StreetKit` and `SM_FloorKit` from the Blender script; user, 2026-11-10: every object as crisp as the kitchen's appliances): market stalls, queue posts and ropes, barriers, scooters, phones, cameras, a ring light, tables and chairs, bottles, lamps, canopies, doors, planters; desks, softboxes, backdrops, screen walls, a safe, vending machines and more. The game's own vendor stalls outside the kitchen use the same stall model.
- **Quitting to here:** the pause menu's "Quit to the main menu" (kitchen or office) loads this scene with `OpeningSequence.StartAtMenu` set: no notice and no approach, a fade in on the entrance with the menu up. Quit on the main menu closes the game.
- **Captures:** *Tools/Game/Capture Office* renders `opening_flight_*`, `opening_menu` (the menu over the stop), `opening_climb_*` at ten moments of the climb as the game plays it (the cab and the helicopter where they'd be), `street_plan` (the street from above) and `look_*_bands*` (the lamp-band trial). For the stills it freezes a believable instant: one flash in nine going off, every other phone popping, the cab and chef where they'd be.

- **Leaving for the kitchen:** `MiniatureTable` in the back room. Once every joined player stands around the table, any of them presses A and the kitchen loads. A hint above the join slots counts who's there ("Everyone to the table: 2 of 3"), then shows "A Start the day". The players walk into the kitchen as they are, on the same controllers and in the same order, so the same colours and chefs, without joining again (`PlayerRoster`, user's call 2026-11-09). A player whose controller went away on the way joins as usual.
- **Boot order** (`BootOrder`, run by the office and menu builders): `Office_Greybox`, then `Kitchen_Greybox`. `MainMenu.unity` stays in the project for the menu's UI captures but isn't in builds.
