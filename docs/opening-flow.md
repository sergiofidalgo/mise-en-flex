# The opening — from launch to the office

*Spec, 2026-11-09, from the user's outline and their answers. References: the skyline mockup (`10_menu_skyline.svg`), the main menu mockup (`Art/Direction/concepts/2026-11-08_main-menu_new-player.png`), `office-scene.md`. Steps 2–9 built in `Office_Greybox`, the boot scene (2026-11-09).*

The first half-minute takes players from the city's streets up the boss's tower and into his floor, in one unbroken camera move. The menu and the office become one place: the game happens in the glowing penthouse you see from the street.

## The city

The fly-through, the menu's resting shot and the office have to be one city:
- **The tower's ground floor** (user, 2026-11-10, third pass):
  - **West, the tower's own lobby:** marble, a reception desk, the lift's glass shaft, the doorman at its door.
  - **East, the boss's restaurant, Mise en Flex,** behind clear glass. By the door, a host and the bar. Up six steps, a raised dining room right behind the windows, so it shows over the heads outside: cloths, candles, couples eating, someone photographing their plate, waiters with trays. At the back, an open kitchen with cooks at the hobs, steam and firelight.
  - **Its sign** is a walnut fascia with brass edges and the name in the logo's serif italic, lit by gooseneck lamps. The neon name, the five stars and the giant feed screen are gone: they didn't sit with the scene.
  - **Outside:** a queue behind a rope, a red carpet under a canopy ringed with bulbs, a couple posing, photographers, an influencer under a ring light, delivery riders by their scooters, people going in and out.
- **Phones first** (user, 2026-11-10: special emphasis on customers using their phones): a chunky modelled phone (a case in seven colours, a lit screen with an app on it, a camera island and the flash's LED on the back) held where it shows, since the characters' arms are short: selfies on a short stick out to the side, filming up in both hands, scrolling with the phone tilted at the chest and its glow on them. The flash is a flare at the phone's own LED.
- **Social pops, sparingly** (user, 2026-11-10: with all of them the scene was too busy): one phone in four pops at all, and rarely: four hearts, a like or rating pill, or a review bubble.
- **Another place to eat** in the podium west of the lobby, with a terrace.
- **A street closed to traffic** between barriers, with a few market stalls among actual restaurants (user, 2026-11-10: too many stalls at first): the corner buildings either side of the avenue's mouth are restaurants with terraces; two stalls stand in the mouth and three along the street; bulbs are strung overhead; selfie pairs stand where the camera passes.
- **The avenue** (the user's diagram, 2026-11-09) is cut back to its mouth: the camera never looks down it now, so only its first buildings remain.
- **The same tower:** the boss's tower, with the penthouse floor as built in `Office_Greybox`. It's glass all the way down, in the city's own style: a stone podium, stone piers, ledges, bronze mullions and louvred plant floors.
- **Floors you can see into** (user, 2026-11-10; "Mixed" chosen):
  - Where the camera climbs (the middle of the front), clear glass shows real rooms. Each floor is a part of the boss's empire (user's choice, 2026-11-10), in turn:
    Every floor is busy (user, 2026-11-10): people sit typing at most desks, others walk the aisle by the window either side of the lift, cooks cross the kitchens, someone talks with their hands, screens and hob flames flicker.
    - a test kitchen with cooks at the hobs (the kitchen's own appliances);
    - a photo studio shooting a dish, its flash going;
    - the social-media room, with walls of screens;
    - the reservations call centre;
    - the accountants, with the safe and the money;
    - the staff canteen;
    - the uniform room;
    - now and then, a floor being fitted out.
  - Every other window paints its room with depth (`SH_InteriorCard`, interior mapping): lit or dark, desks, someone at the back, blinds, a TV's flicker.
- **The lift is inside** (user, 2026-11-10): a glass cab between bronze posts, in the same place as the boss's floor's elevator, seen through the glass on the way up.
- **The sky** (user, 2026-11-09: "the sky is super important too"): stars that twinkle, a big moon with a halo in the open sky beside the tower's top, slow clouds silvered near the moon, and airliners crossing high over the city, blinking.
- **Hustle all along the avenue** (user, 2026-11-09), dense with clutter, movement and detail, built from the city kit and the office's `Ambience` life:
  - **clutter:** parked cars and scooters, bins and bin bags, newspaper boxes, fire hydrants, café tables, crates;
  - **movement:** people walking and waiting, a delivery rider, cars passing with their lights, pigeons, steam from a manhole;
  - **detail:** shop awnings and lit shopfronts, neon signs, fire escapes, string lights, posters;
  - **signs:** blade signs over the sidewalks, with neon words (BAR, NOODLES, HOTEL...) or lit boxes with a logo (a ramen bowl, a cocktail, a slice...); wall neon with made-up shop names and icons, its colour washing the wall; "OPEN" and "WE'RE OPEN" in the shop windows;
  - **people:** every 24 m a moment: a selfie, a portrait on a phone, friends chatting, a queue behind a rope, a tourist photographing the tower; flashes going off; a food market on a little square; twice as many walkers;
  - **cars:** sedans, hatchbacks, taxis and vans (`SM_Cars`), the moving ones lighting the road ahead;
  - **graffiti:** tags and murals on shutters and side walls, painted textures in the game's colours, never real-world logos or words that read as brands.
- **The office's city** (the near roofs and their life) and the menu's city become one layout seen from two heights, not two different cities.

## The hand-over (step 7)

**One scene** (user, 2026-11-09): the office scene holds the street, the menu and the floor, so there's nothing to load between them. The climb runs from wherever the camera is drifting at the stop, up the tower's glass, and turns onto the office camera's pre-join wide shot. The first thing players see is the whole floor and the elevator they'll come out of.

## How it's built (2026-11-09, reworked 2026-11-10)

- **`OpeningSequence`** (Presentation/Office) runs the steps:
  1. save notice (first launch only, a `PlayerPrefs` flag; A skips it);
  2. 0.5 s fade in;
  3. the approach, 8 s (A skips, every time);
  4. a 2 s beat, then the menu fades in, the camera still;
  5. the chef walks in, about 6 s, the camera still (A skips to the floor);
  6. the climb, 12 s (A skips to the floor);
  7. land.

  On landing, the follow camera takes over and joining opens. The menu's controller joins as player 1, coming out of the floor's elevator: the chef who rode up. The cab's chef hides and the empty cab sinks back down. The street level, the tower's rooms and the lift switch off, since nobody sees them from the floor. The floor's player bar (`PlayerBarHud`) is hidden until this moment.
- **`OpeningChef`**: player 1's chef, in their look from the tailor (`ChefProfileFile`) and their colour, waits at the kerb. They walk a set path through the tower's door, round the shaft to the cab's doors (which slide open), and ride up.
- **The camera path** is a centripetal Catmull-Rom curve through poses set in `OfficeBuilder.Opening.cs` (`OpeningPath`, tested). It's sampled by distance, so the pace is even however far apart the poses are. The approach is two poses looking the same way, so it's a straight push with no turn. It runs 2.8 m east of the tower's middle, so the restaurant fills the frame beside the menu. The climb goes straight up from where the camera stopped, about 16 m off the glass, then over the cornice.
- **The lift** (`OpeningElevator`) rides level with the camera on the climb, so the chef stays in the middle of the frame.
- **The haze** is thinner (170–650 m) in the street and over the menu, and eases back to the office's on the climb, so the tower reads from the street.
- **`OpeningHud`**: the black veil for fades, the save card (turning icon and two lines), "A Skip" bottom right, and the four join slots in player colours along the bottom.
- **`MainMenu`** starts hidden in the office (`_startHidden`). When the office listens to `Launching`, the menu hands over instead of loading the kitchen.
- **The modelled kit** (`Kit.cs`; `SM_StreetKit` and `SM_FloorKit` from the Blender script; user, 2026-11-10: every object as crisp as the kitchen's appliances): market stalls, queue posts and ropes, barriers, scooters, phones, cameras, a ring light, tables and chairs, bottles, lamps, canopies, doors, planters; desks, softboxes, backdrops, screen walls, a safe, vending machines and more. The game's own vendor stalls outside the kitchen use the same stall model.
- **Quitting to here:** the pause menu's "Quit to the main menu" (kitchen or office) loads this scene with `OpeningSequence.StartAtMenu` set: no notice and no approach, a fade in on the entrance with the menu up. Quit on the main menu closes the game.
- **Captures:** *Tools/Game/Capture Office* renders `opening_flight_*`, `opening_menu` (the menu over the stop), `opening_entering_*`, `opening_climb_*` and `opening_climbing_*` along the path. For the stills it freezes a believable instant: one flash in five going off, every other phone popping, the cab and chef where they'd be.

- **Leaving for the kitchen:** `MiniatureTable` in the back room. Once every joined player stands around the table, any of them presses A and the kitchen loads. A hint above the join slots counts who's there ("Everyone to the table: 2 of 3"), then shows "A Start the day". The players walk into the kitchen as they are, on the same controllers and in the same order, so the same colours and chefs, without joining again (`PlayerRoster`, user's call 2026-11-09). A player whose controller went away on the way joins as usual.
- **Boot order** (`BootOrder`, run by the office and menu builders): `Office_Greybox`, then `Kitchen_Greybox`. `MainMenu.unity` stays in the project for the menu's UI captures but isn't in builds.
