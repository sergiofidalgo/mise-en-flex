# Phase 3 plan: The daily loop and staff

*Agreed 2026-11-03. Decisions in `../decisions.md`.*

**Done when:** you play several days in a row. You plan, prep, serve, close and review; stock and money carry over between days; and hiring someone visibly takes work off your hands.

## Decisions

- **Waiter and runner move to Phase 4,** with tables: at a counter, they'd have nothing to do.
- **Prep has no time limit;** it ends when all players ready up.
- **Close is summarised,** with a line for waste and spoilage.
- **Staff have fixed roles.**
- **Restocking is shopping:** purchases go to the fridge and shelf (a free morning delivery for now). Trays carry stock to containers on counters.
- **Hiring is a menu in Plan:** 2–3 candidates per role, described in words, paid daily. You can't hire what you can't afford.
- **Progress lasts for the session;** saves come in Phase 5. Ingredient costs, prices and debt also wait for Phase 5.

## How we'll know it's fun

- You feel the difference between a day you prepped well and one you didn't.
- You feel relief the first time someone else does the washing up.
- A hire is a real decision, because the wage has to be worth it.
